Setup

Settings

Your RSS feeds

Paste any RSS, Atom, or JSON listing. Channel-list apps, sports blogs, or a homemade XML file all work. Core Line never plays video — it only reads names and channels.

On your phone, same Wi-Fi as this TV. Scan or open the address. Type the code. Do not type the RSS URL on the Stick.

    Scoreboards

    Checked leagues are fetched from public scoreboards and shown as their own tabs. Unchecked leagues are hidden but stay available to re-enable.

    Ticker & display

    Screen fit

    Classic TVs crop the edge of the picture. Calibrate once and Core Line keeps everything inside the frame.

    Floating ticker

    Draws the crawl on top of every app, edge to edge. Stop it from the notification or untick this box.

    VPN status dot

    Android TV has no status bar, so once you are watching something there is no way to tell whether your VPN is still up. This draws one small dot in a corner of every app until you switch it off.

    Checking the VPN…

    Green means a tunnel is up and Core Line is inside it. Amber means a tunnel is up but this app is outside it (per-app VPN). Red means there is no tunnel.

    Favorite teams

    A short alert on the crawl when a favorite team scores or takes the lead.

    Open games in other apps

    Assign an installed app to each league. Pressing ▶ Watch on a game (or the W key) opens that app so you can watch; “Web browser” opens the game page on ESPN instead.

    IPTV playlist (M3U)

    Import the M3U link from your IPTV provider (Xtream “m3u_plus” links work too). Game details then list your channels that carry the game, and open them in your player app — Core Line itself never plays video.

    The link is fetched by Core Line on this device and never leaves it. Opening a channel needs a player that accepts stream links — TiviMate, VLC, or similar.

    Updates

    Core Line updates come from the GitHub release feed. On a TV, download the new APK and the system installer takes over — your settings are kept.

    Help & about

    Core Line reads public scoreboards (ESPN, NHL, MLB) and your own RSS/JSON feeds. It never plays video — the Watch feature hands a game off to the app you choose.

    D-pad: move · OK: select · Air mouse: point & click · S: settings · T: ticker · R: refresh · W: watch · /: search · F: fullscreen.

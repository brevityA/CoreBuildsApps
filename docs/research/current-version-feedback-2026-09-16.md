# Current-version icon feedback

**Date:** 2026-09-16 (Australia/Sydney)
**Reported against:** Core Builds Icon Pack v1.8.19

## Nuvio TV

Feedback: the small play button inside the Nuvio mark appeared slightly too far
right. The inner play triangle has been moved left so its visual centre is
centred within the outer play wedge. This is a geometry-only correction; the
Nuvio gradient and mapping are unchanged.

The Classic, Pop, and Pixel Neon derived assets must be regenerated after the
change, and the corrected mark still requires launcher/card-size review.

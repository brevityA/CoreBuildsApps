adb_guide_body = First unlock the developer options:

1. Settings, then System, then About
2. Press Build number seven times
3. Go back and open Developer options

What comes next depends on what your TV offers there:

A plain on/off switch (Network debugging, ADB debugging or ADB over network): turn it on, come back here and press Connect. No computer needed.

Only Wireless debugging with a pairing code: Android does not let an app pair itself. Do it once from a computer on the same network with adb pair, then adb tcpip 5555, and press Connect here afterwards.

Your TV then asks whether to allow debugging. Choose Always allow, and that is it.

Tip: run Give Monet system rights right away. Those rights stay after a restart, even when the debugging port closes again.

It is free, the connection stays on this device and nothing is sent anywhere.

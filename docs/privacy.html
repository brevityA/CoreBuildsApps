Core Builds Icon Pack — Privacy Policy

The app has no account and collects nothing in the background. This page is the complete account of what leaves your device, when, and where it goes.

Applies to: Core Builds Icon Pack, package tv.corebuilds.iconpack (GitHub / Downloader build) and tv.corebuilds.iconpack.play (Google Play build), including the companion Core Builds Glyphs Pack (tv.corebuilds.iconpack.glyphs and tv.corebuilds.iconpack.glyphs.play, which have no network code and send nothing) and Core Builds Glyphs (tv.corebuilds.glyphs, the icon pack drawn in glyph art, covered wherever this page says "the app").

Effective: 28 October 2026.

There is no account, and no profile

The app has no sign-in, no user identifier and no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting library. It does not read your advertising ID, your contacts, your location, your files or your installed-app list beyond the launcher query described below. Nothing is collected in the background.

No third party receives anything else. There is no advertising network, no data broker, no analytics provider and no server of ours that stores your activity. The broker, when it is enabled, keeps your IP address for at most 62 minutes to rate-limit requests, never writes it to the issue, and keeps nothing else beyond the Premium hash described above. Its code is public: tools/icon_request_broker/ in the repository.

What the app reads on your device

To tell you which of your apps the icon pack covers, the auditor asks Android which apps have a launcher entry. It reads app names and component names to show you that list. That list stays on your device; it is sent nowhere, in whole or in part, unless you press Request icon for one specific app, which sends only that app's name and component.

What the app writes

If you use Export, wallpaper masters are copied into Pictures/CoreBuilds on your own storage so a launcher such as Monet can rotate them. Downloaded wallpapers and loops are cached in the app's private storage. On the Google Play build the app also keeps the Core Premium purchase token, if you own Premium; the time you started its 24-hour trial, if you did; and for My requests the list of requests filed from this TV (up to 200: each app's name, component and issue number, all of which are already public in the issue). Live loop rotation, the night loop, the Projectivy walls settings and the trial run on the TV and send nothing. Everything in the app's private storage is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Exported wallpapers are ordinary files in your Pictures folder: they stay there until you delete them.

Permissions, and why each exists

  • Internet — to download wallpapers, loops and (on the GitHub build) the update check.
  • Network state — to report "no connection" instead of failing silently when you tap a wallpaper.
  • Set wallpaper — to apply a wallpaper you choose.
  • Write external storage (Android 9 and older only) — for the Pictures export on those versions.
  • Install unknown apps — GitHub / Downloader build only, for its own self-update and the Glyphs companion. The Play build removes this permission entirely.
  • Google Play billing — Google Play build only, to sell and confirm the Core Premium purchase.

Purchases

The GitHub / Downloader build has no purchases. The Google Play build can offer one: Core Premium, an optional one-time purchase (no subscription) that adds priority icon requests, My requests, live loop rotation, the night loop, the choice of what Projectivy rotates from the pack, and the Spectra and Aurum wallpapers. It is offered only in builds where the request broker is enabled, because priority requests need it. Until then the Play build offers no purchases.

Where it is offered, each TV can try it free for 24 hours, once. The trial is kept on the TV by the app's own clock: starting it sends nothing, involves no payment detail, and ends by itself. It does not include priority requests or saving Spectra and Aurum walls to Pictures, which come with the purchase.

Payment is processed by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. No card or billing detail, Google account name or e-mail address ever reaches the app or the broker: the app learns only whether this TV's Google account owns Premium, and keeps the purchase token Play gives it. Play Console gives developers an order record (order number, product, price, date, country), which Core Builds uses only for refunds and tax. Refunds go through Google Play.

Children

The app is a wallpaper and icon pack. It has no social features, no user content, no chat and no advertising, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your control

On the TV, clearing the app's data removes its settings, cache, request list and Premium token, and the record of a trial (so clearing it offers the trial again). Off the TV, only three things can exist, and only after you press Request icon:

  • The public issue. Filed from your browser, it is yours: you can edit it from your GitHub account, but GitHub lets only the repository's owner delete an issue. Filed by the broker, it carries no account or contact detail, so it cannot be looked up by person. Either way, open an issue with its number and it is deleted.
  • Your IP address, held by the broker for at most 62 minutes for rate limiting. It expires by itself and is never written anywhere else.
  • A SHA-256 hash of a Premium purchase token, held by the broker with Google's answer for at most six hours. It expires by itself.

Nothing else about you is kept, so there is nothing else to request, correct or export.

Changes to this policy

If a future version sends anything the table above does not list, this page grows a row for it before that version ships, and the effective date changes. The app's own About screen carries the same complete list, so the two can be compared rather than trusted.
